Novarena

A turn-based tactics game: heroes from every era of fantasy, pulled into one arena.

Early days

Novarena is in early development. For now you can play battles on an open arena, with a sample battle or units you choose for each side: move each of your party into place, pick an ability and a foe in reach (or an ally to heal), and watch the foes answer. Every hit lands for a fixed amount; nothing is left to chance. Settings choose whether turns end by themselves, how foes' turns play, and whether your units counter spells.

Your data

Novarena doesn't collect anything; your settings stay on your iPhone. See the privacy policy.
