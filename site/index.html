Novarena

A turn-based tactics game: heroes from every era of fantasy, pulled into one arena.

Early days

Novarena is in early development. For now you can play one sample battle on an open arena: move each of your party into place, pick an ability and a foe in reach, and watch the foes answer. Every hit lands for a fixed amount; nothing is left to chance.
