Privacy policy

Novarena

What the app collects

Nothing. Novarena has no account, no server, no analytics and no ads. It doesn't track you or share anything with anyone.

Where your data lives

For now Novarena stores nothing. If it later saves your progress, it will stay on your iPhone, and this page will say so.

Crash reports

If you've chosen to share analytics with app developers in the iPhone Settings app, Apple may pass on anonymous crash reports and usage numbers. You can turn this off in Settings, Privacy & Security, Analytics & Improvements.

Changes and contact

If this policy changes, this page will say so. Questions? Open an issue on GitHub.
